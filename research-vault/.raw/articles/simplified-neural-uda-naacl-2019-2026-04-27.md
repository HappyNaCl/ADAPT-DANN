# Simplified Neural Unsupervised Domain Adaptation

**Author:** Timothy Miller
**Year:** 2019
**Venue:** NAACL 2019

Simplifies neural UDA by removing dependence on manually-selected pivot features required by prior SOTA (e.g., SCL). Jointly optimizes representation learning and task learning. Pre-BERT era; uses BiLSTM + adversarial domain discriminator. Eliminates the pivot feature selection step that made SCL-based approaches brittle. Competitive results with simpler design. Shows that end-to-end joint training subsumes the pivot selection heuristic.
