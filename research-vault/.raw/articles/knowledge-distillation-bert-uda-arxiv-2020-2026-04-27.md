# Knowledge Distillation for BERT Unsupervised Domain Adaptation

**Authors:** Minho Ryu, Kichun Lee
**Year:** 2020
**Venue:** arXiv 2020 (cs.CL)

Proposes AAD (Adversarial Adaptation with Distillation): merges ADDA framework with knowledge distillation to mitigate domain shift in BERT. ADDA handles feature-level alignment; KD transfers task knowledge from source-adapted teacher to target-domain student. Evaluated on cross-domain sentiment classification across 30 domain pairs. Achieves SOTA at time of publication. Key contribution: KD provides a soft supervision signal that stabilizes the otherwise brittle ADDA adversarial training on BERT.
