# UDALM: Unsupervised Domain Adaptation through Language Modeling

**Authors:** Constantinos Karouzos, Georgios Paraskevopoulos, Alexandros Potamianos
**Year:** 2021
**Venue:** NAACL 2021

Proposes UDA through language modeling objectives on target domain text, combined with adversarial training to reduce distribution mismatch. Bridges pre-trained LMs with adversarial feature alignment: continues LM on target domain (akin to DAPT) then applies adversarial discriminator. Evaluated on cross-domain sentiment classification. Key finding: LM adaptation + adversarial training is complementary — DAPT-style pretraining and DANN-style alignment can be stacked.
