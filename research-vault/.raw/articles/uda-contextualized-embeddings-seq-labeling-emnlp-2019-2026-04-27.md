# Unsupervised Domain Adaptation of Contextualized Embeddings for Sequence Labeling

**Authors:** Xiaochuang Han, Jacob Eisenstein
**Year:** 2019
**Venue:** EMNLP-IJCNLP 2019

Proposes domain-adaptive fine-tuning: continue masked language modeling (MLM) on unlabeled target domain text to adapt BERT/ELMo embeddings. Evaluated on sequence labeling tasks in Early Modern English and Twitter. Demonstrates substantial improvements over base BERT, particularly for out-of-vocabulary words in linguistically distant domains. An early, influential instantiation of DAPT specifically for sequence labeling — predates and anticipates Gururangan et al. 2020's systematic DAPT study.
