# Self-Adaptation for Unsupervised Domain Adaptation

**Authors:** Xia Cui, Danushka Bollegala
**Year:** 2019
**Venue:** RANLP 2019

Proposes a self-adaptation mechanism where models adjust their own representations and predictions based on target domain characteristics without labeled target data. Pre-BERT era. Models iteratively update their feature representations using target domain signals. Relates to self-training but focuses on representation adaptation rather than label propagation. Evaluated on sentiment classification across domains.
