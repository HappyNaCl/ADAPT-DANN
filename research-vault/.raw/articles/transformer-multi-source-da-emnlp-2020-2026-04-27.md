# Transformer Based Multi-Source Domain Adaptation

**Authors:** Dustin Wright, Isabelle Augenstein
**Year:** 2020
**Venue:** EMNLP 2020

Addresses unsupervised multi-source domain adaptation using transformer models. Examines mixture of experts and domain adversarial training — techniques proven effective with CNNs/RNNs — when applied to large pretrained transformers. Finds transformer-based domain experts produce highly homogenous predictions, creating challenges for effective prediction-mixing metrics. One novel metric based on attention is proposed. Combining predictions from multiple domain expert classifiers improves performance, but transformers show inherent cross-domain robustness that reduces the gap between single and multi-source DA.
